<h1 align="center">KYBERTIS</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>The software is open. The accountability is ours.</strong></p>

KYBERTIS is the company behind the open Kyber systems. The code is open and free to run yourself. What we sell is getting it into production in your business, keeping it there and being the name on the contract: most often KYBERIOS on your own server, on a hardened KyberLinux, with written rules for your agents, KyberDeck devices for your team, and support with an SLA and an exit clause.

## What we do

- **Deployment** of KYBERIOS on your own server.
- **Hardening of KyberLinux.**
- **Rules for your agents**, in writing, plus devices and identities for your team.
- **Support with an SLA**, and an exit in the contract.
